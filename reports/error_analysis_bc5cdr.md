## 7. Factual Takeaways & Architectural Insights

1. **Chemical Extraction is Substantially More Robust than Disease:** Chemical F1 reaches **83.09%** with 81.60% Precision and 84.63% Recall, whereas Disease F1 is **68.98%** (Precision 66.81%, Recall 71.30%).
2. **Primary Driver of Disease Errors:** Complex multi-word clinical syndromic descriptions and modifier attachment (e.g. omitting 'acute', 'familial', 'induced') constitute the primary failure mode.
3. **Chemical vs Disease Confusion is Low:** The model rarely misclassifies an exact chemical span as disease (only 7 instances out of 1,488 entities). Misclassifications are predominantly boundary truncations and omissions.
