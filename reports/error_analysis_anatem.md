## 7. Factual Takeaways & Architectural Insights

1. **Highest Precision Across Benchmark:** AnatEM achieves **81.44%** Precision and **79.59%** F1, representing the cleanest boundary adherence among all 4 benchmarks.
2. **Conservative Prediction Behavior:** The model generated fewer entities (1,056) than ground truth (1,105), resulting in low false positive rates and strong reliability on anatomical structures.
3. **Cellular vs Organ Ambiguity:** Rare errors occur on micro-anatomical structures (e.g. cellular organelles vs tissue vs macro-anatomy).
